# Platform copy

The copy is part of the hook, not decoration added afterwards.

## Procedure

1. **Open on the same promise the video opens on.** If the first frame states an outcome,
   the first line of the caption states the same outcome. A caption that sets up a different
   expectation costs retention.
2. **Front-load.** Most platforms truncate. The first ~60 characters must stand alone.
3. **Hashtags: 3–5, specific over broad.** One community tag, one topic tag, one format tag.
   Twenty tags signals spam and dilutes reach. Never invent a branded tag that does not exist.
4. **On-screen text is not the caption.** Short, high-contrast, inside the safe area, and it
   must survive being read with sound off.
5. **Match the platform's register.** TikTok tolerates informality; Shorts skews
   instructional; Reels sits between. Do not write the same copy three times and relabel it.

## Output

`caption` · `hashtags[]` · `onScreenText[]` (one per segment, optional).

## Rules

- **No claim in the caption that the video does not show.** No invented statistics, no
  invented testimonials, no "everyone is switching to".
- No emoji as a substitute for a word that carries meaning.
- Do not promise a link, a discount, or a follow-up that does not exist.
- Copy is package-ready output. **Producing copy is not permission to post it.**
